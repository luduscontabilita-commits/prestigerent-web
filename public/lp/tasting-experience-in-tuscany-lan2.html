Start your adventure

Meet your expert guide in Florence and leave the city bustle behind. As we drive 45 minutes into the rolling hills of Chianti, you’ll be introduced to the history and “Black Rooster” legends of Italy’s most famous Chianti region.

Stop 1: The ancient traditions

Our first stop takes you into the heart of a historic estate. Walk through age-old barrels, breathe in the scent of aging oak, and learn the secrets of the winemaking process. Your visit concludes with a guided tasting of premium Chianti selections and fresh olive oil.

Stop 2: Views & Tuscan flavors

A short, scenic drive leads to our second hilltop estate. Here, the focus is on the nuances between different blends. Put your new skills to the test with a second tasting, perfectly paired with a selection of local specialties: savory salami, aged pecorino cheese, and crispy bruschetta.

Back to Florence

Enjoy the final views of vineyards and olive groves as we head back to Florence, arriving just in time for your lunch (AM tour) or dinner (PM tour).

FAQ’s
What happens if the weather looks bad?
Rain or Shine Guarantee (Zero Risk)

In Tuscany, rainy days are rare, and our tours run beautifully regardless of the weather. Our modern, climate-controlled vehicles are fully enclosed and comfortable, and your tastings take place inside cozy, historic cellars.

Maximum Flexibility & Peace of Mind

We pride ourselves on our flexibility and reasonable approach. Check the forecast up to 24 hours before your departure—if you see rain and prefer to switch dates, we will do everything possible to accommodate you on another day, completely free of charge. We are humans, not rigid corporations: your peace of mind is our priority.

Is this a “Big Bus” tour?

Absolutely not. While standard tours run with 50/60+ people, we keep our groups to a maximum of 25 for the small group option or 8 for the semiprivate one. This ensures a safe, intimate, and comfortable experience.

How long is the tour?

This tour takes about 4.5 hours so you can use the balance of the day for other activities in the morning or afternoon. The morning tour ends at approx 1:45pm; the afternoon tour ends at approx 7:00pm.

How many wineries are we going to visit?

We will visit 2 of Tuscany’s finest wineries: usually Montecchio and Casa Emma. However, in case of unavailability or unforeseeable circumstances, we will visit comparable premium wineries.

Are tasting fees included?

Yes, all tasting fees at the 2 estates are included on this tour.

Is food included?

This is a tasting-focused tour. While not a full meal, we provide a generous “Tuscan Snack” at the second estate (cheese, salami, bruschetta) to perfectly complement the tastings.

Do we taste white varieties?

This tour includes 2 different estates located in the Chianti Classico area, famous for reds. As a plus, depending on the time of the year, production, etc., you may also taste some rosé or a white variety (not guaranteed).

What should I bring?

A bottle of water, all you think is necessary according to the forecast, and money or a credit card if you want to buy/ship bottles, olive oil, etc.

Can I buy or ship bottles and olive oil?

Yes! You can buy a few bottles or a larger quantity to ship home. Make sure you know your country’s rules of taking bottles back home – every country has different rules.

Can I join the tour with an infant/child younger than 12?

Unfortunately, no. We love infants/children however, to maintain a sophisticated atmosphere for all guests, this tour is reserved for travelers aged 12 and up. As an alternative, please have a look at our private version of the Tuscan tasting tour.
