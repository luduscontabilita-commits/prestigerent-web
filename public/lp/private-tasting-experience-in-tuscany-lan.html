A private wine tour from Florence to Chianti

Chianti begins twenty minutes out of Florence and does not really end. Between the two you cross the landscape people picture when they picture Italy: vineyards in rows down the slopes, olive groves, cypresses on the ridge, stone farmhouses that have been there for four hundred years. With a private car you stop when you want to look at it.

The Chianti Wine Road and Greve in Chianti

The road winds between the estates. Your first stop is Greve in Chianti, the working heart of Chianti Classico — not a museum village. Piazza Matteotti slopes gently under its porticoes, lined with wine shops and food shops, and in the middle stands Giovanni da Verrazzano, the local man who sailed into New York harbour and got a bridge named after him. The Falorni butcher's has been on this square for five hundred years; go in, if only for the smell.

Into the heart of Chianti Classico

Onwards past Panzano in Chianti, its church watching the valley, and into the San Donato area — the centre of the Chianti Classico appellation, where the gallo nero, the black rooster, on a bottle actually means something.

The winery visit and tasting

You stop at a hilltop estate for a visit and a tasting: Chianti wines, and the estate's own extra virgin olive oil. This is included in your price. Because the day is private, the tasting is pitched at you — if one of you knows their Sangiovese and another has never got past supermarket red, they can say so, and somebody will actually answer.

Want more? A second tasting is €40 per person, or €70 with a light lunch. Ask when you book so the estate expects you.

And then the day is yours

This is the part a coach cannot offer. Stop for a long lunch in a trattoria where nobody is waiting for you. Visit a second or third estate. Drive on to Castellina in Chianti, Radda in Chianti or the walled village of Monteriggioni. Or simply stay where you are, because the view is better than the schedule. Tell your driver in the morning, or tell us when you book and we will prepare it.

Who books a private Chianti wine tour

Couples on an anniversary who would rather not share the cellar with thirty strangers. Families where the grandparents need the car close and the children need the day to flex. Groups of friends splitting one price. People who want to buy wine seriously and take it with them. Travellers with limited mobility. And anyone who has worked out the obvious: on a wine day, the best thing money buys is somebody else at the wheel.

FAQ’s
Is this wine tour really private? Who else will be in the car?

Your party is the only one in the vehicle. Nobody else is ever added to your booking. It is your family or your friends, your driver, and the Chianti Wine Road — which is the whole point of a private wine tour from Florence rather than a shared departure.

How much does the private Chianti tour cost, and is it per person?

It is per party, not per person, so the more of you there are the less each one pays: €590 for one guest, €680 for two, €770 for three, €860 for four, €950 for five, €1,040 for six, €1,130 for seven and €1,220 for eight. Vehicle, fluent English-speaking driver, the winery visit and tasting, fuel, tolls and parking are all in. Pick your date and party size in the calendar and you will see your exact price before paying anything.

Is the wine tasting included, or do we pay at the winery?

One winery visit with tasting is included in the price — Chianti wines and the estate's own Tuscan olive oil. Nothing to pay on arrival for that one. If you would like more, an additional tasting is €40 per person, or €70 per person with a light lunch. Book those in advance: the estates need to know you are coming.

How many wineries do we visit?

One is included, and the rest of the day is open. Because it is private, you decide on the spot: a second or third estate, a long lunch in a trattoria, or the villages — Castellina in Chianti, Radda in Chianti, Monteriggioni. Tell us when you book if you already know, or tell your driver on the morning.

We are not staying in Florence. Can you still collect us?

Yes. We are a Florence company but we drive the whole country, so we come and get you wherever you are in Italy — a villa in Chianti or the Val d'Orcia, a hotel in Siena, Pisa, Lucca, Montecatini or Rome, a cruise ship in Livorno or Civitavecchia, an arrival at Florence, Pisa or Rome airport.

Which vehicle will we get? Can we choose the car?

It is always a Mercedes, assigned to the size of your party: an E-Class or S-Class sedan for up to 4 guests, a V-Class for up to 6, a Sprinter minivan for up to 8. Can we buy wine and have it shipped home?

Yes, and this is where a private day pays for itself. The estates sell their own wine and olive oil, and most ship internationally — the United States, Canada, the UK, Australia. And because the car is yours, the boot is yours: no coach rules, no baggage allowance, no carrying crates back to a meeting point.

What if some of us do not drink wine?

Nobody has to taste anything. Tell us when you book: the estate will put out olive oil, balsamic vinegar and something to eat, and the day can lean more towards the villages and the countryside. Children are welcome and travel in the same car with you — child seats on request.

Does the driver speak English? Is he also our guide?

Your driver speaks fluent English and drives these roads every week: which estate suits your taste, where to stop for the photograph, which trattoria is worth it. Is the private wine tour suitable for guests with reduced mobility?

Yes, and it is often the right choice for exactly that reason: the car is always a few steps away, there is no group pace to keep and nobody waiting on you. We can carry foldable wheelchairs and scooters — tell us at the time of booking and send the folded dimensions, so we can be sure they fit.

What should we bring, and what happens if it rains?

Comfortable shoes, and that is about it. The tour runs rain or shine and the car is never more than a few steps away. Check the forecast up to 24 hours before departure — if you would rather move to another day we will do everything we can, free of charge, and cancellation up to 24 hours before is free with a full refund.
