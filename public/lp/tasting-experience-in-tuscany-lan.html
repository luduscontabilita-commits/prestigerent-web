Why you'll love it

The highlights

  • Small group, max 25 (or just 8 semiprivate) — while others pack 50+ people on a bus, we keep it intimate and relaxed, in English only: no confusing translations
  • Dual winery experience — compare two distinct estates, from ancient cellars to modern family-run vineyards, with a generous tasting of premium Chianti wines
  • Beyond wine — taste authentic extra virgin olive oil and rare balsamic vinegar, paired with local Tuscan snacks: salami, aged pecorino cheese and crispy bruschetta
  • The perfect half-day escape — savor the best of Tuscany in about 4.5 hours, with charming views over the Chianti countryside and free cancellation up to 24 hours
The itinerary

Your day, step by step

Start your adventure

Meet your expert guide in Florence and leave the city bustle behind. As we drive 45 minutes into the rolling hills of Chianti, you’ll be introduced to the history and “Black Rooster” legends of Italy’s most famous wine region.

Stop 1: The ancient traditions

Our first stop takes you into the heart of a historic estate. Walk through age-old barrels, breathe in the scent of aging oak, and learn the secrets of the winemaking process. Your visit concludes with a guided tasting of premium Chianti wines and fresh olive oil.

Stop 2: Views & Tuscan flavors

A short, scenic drive leads to our second hilltop estate. Here, the focus is on the nuances between different blends. Put your new skills to the test with a second tasting, perfectly paired with a selection of local specialties: savory salami, aged pecorino cheese, and crispy bruschetta.

Back to Florence

Enjoy the final views of vineyards and olive groves as we head back to Florence, arriving just in time for your lunch (AM tour) or dinner (PM tour).

“The perfect balance of sightseeing and sipping. Book your morning or afternoon departure today and see why this is Florence’s favorite wine escape!”

Meeting point, check-in and return

Check-in time: 8:45am (if booked for the 9:00am tour) or 2:15pm (if booked for the 2:30pm tour). Late arrivals are considered no show.

Meeting point: Piazzale Montelungo (Montelungo Square/bus terminal) – opposite the parking lot. It takes approx. 8 minutes by walk from the Florence Santa Maria Novella Train Station. If you are not familiar with the area, please allow plenty of time.

Please note: the meeting point is just a bus terminal with no facilities, so if you want a coffee, use the restroom, do it in advance.

How to recognize your tour: Look for a red flag/check-in table, with Prestige Rent logo. If you arrive in advance (good idea!) and you cannot recognize your guide, do not panic: your guide will be there at check-in time.

Return time: the morning tour ends at the meeting point around 2:00pm and the afternoon tour around 7:00pm. If you book other activities, restaurants, etc., please allow plenty of time.

IMPORTANT: We cannot hold the tour for late arrivals, it would be disrespectful to the other guests. If you need more info, contact us in advance and not at the last minute.

Good to know before you book

Super friendly cancellation policy: free cancellation up to 24 hours prior departure time.

Group size: tour runs with a minimum of 6 participants.

Age: infants and younger than 12 are not allowed on the small group option; the semiprivate option is for adults only (over 18). Contact us for alternative options.

Activity level: very easy. Limited walking and few stairs. However, due to uneven surfaces and stairs, the tour is not suitable for people with wheelchairs or walking disabilities.

Driving time: about 45 mins each way, for a total of 90 mins.

Sightseeing time: approx. 3 hours (1.5 hrs at each winery).

Wines tasted: reds which are typical of the Chianti region (Chianti vintage, Riserva, Super Tuscan, etc.). As a plus, depending on the time of the year, production, wineries, etc. you may also taste some rosé or white wine (not guaranteed).

Dietary friendly: vegetarian or gluten-free? Just let us know at booking; we’ll ensure your snacks are just as delicious.

Luggage: you can bring your luggage and store it safely on our vehicle during the tour.

Restroom: the bus does not have restrooms, however the longest ride on this tour is approx 45 minutes.

Language: the tour is in English only. No confusion with multiple languages.

What to wear: tour runs rain or shine, please dress accordingly.

Wine shipping: fall in love with a bottle? All wineries offer easy international shipping directly to your doorstep.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What happens if the weather looks bad?
Rain or Shine Guarantee (Zero Risk)

In Tuscany, rainy days are rare, and our tours run beautifully regardless of the weather. Our modern, climate-controlled vehicles are fully enclosed and comfortable, and your wine tastings take place inside cozy, historic cellars.

Maximum Flexibility & Peace of Mind

We pride ourselves on our flexibility and reasonable approach. Check the forecast up to 24 hours before your departure—if you see rain and prefer to switch dates, we will do everything possible to accommodate you on another day, completely free of charge. We are humans, not rigid corporations: your peace of mind is our priority.

Is this a “Big Bus” tour?

Absolutely not. While standard tours run with 50/60+ people, we keep our groups to a maximum of 25 for the small group option or 8 for the semiprivate one. This ensures a safe, intimate, and comfortable experience.

How long is the tour?

This tour takes about 4.5 hours so you can use the balance of the day for other activities in the morning or afternoon. The morning tour ends at approx 1:45pm; the afternoon tour ends at approx 7:00pm.

How many wineries are we going to visit?

We will visit 2 of Tuscany’s finest wineries: usually Montecchio and Casa Emma. However, in case of unavailability or unforeseeable circumstances, we will visit comparable premium wineries.

Are wine tastings included?

Yes, all wine tasting fees at the 2 wineries are included on this tour.

Is food included?

This is a tasting-focused tour. While not a full meal, we provide a generous “Tuscan Snack” at the second winery (cheese, salami, bruschetta) to perfectly complement the wines.

Do we taste white wines?

This tour includes 2 different wineries located in the Chianti Classico area, famous for red wines. As a plus, depending on the time of the year, production, etc., you may also taste some rosé or white wine (not guaranteed).

What should I bring?

A bottle of water, all you think is necessary according to the forecast, and money or a credit card if you want to buy/ship wine, olive oil, etc.

Can I buy or ship wine and olive oil?

Yes! You can buy a few bottles or a larger quantity to ship home. Make sure you know your country’s rules of taking wine back home – every country has different rules.

Can I join the tour with an infant/child younger than 12?

Unfortunately, no. We love infants/children however, to maintain a sophisticated atmosphere for all guests, this tour is reserved for travelers aged 12 and up. As an alternative, please have a look at our private version of the Tuscan wine tour.
