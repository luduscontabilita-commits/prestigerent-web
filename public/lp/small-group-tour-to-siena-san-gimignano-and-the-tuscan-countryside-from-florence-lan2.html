Enjoy a day trip to Siena & San Gimignano

Join our small-group tour to visit 2 Tuscan gems and an ancient winery: the perfect mix of art, history, gorgeous Tuscan views, food and wine!

See the highlights of Siena on a guided walking tour, admire the marvelous Tuscan landscape, taste genuine local food paired with great wines and finish your day relaxing in the hilltop village of San Gimignano.

Guided visit to Siena

Discover the beautiful art city of Siena with a knowledgeable guide will take you on a walk through the town's picturesque narrow streets. See the shell-shaped Piazza del Campo, one of the most beautiful squares in Italy, a brick-paved square where the famous 'Palio' horserace is held twice a year.

Admire the magnificent Cathedral and the tall and trim Torre del Mangia. After the guided tour, enjoy your free time to wonder Siena at your own pace, to shop around or have an Italian espresso.

Tuscan countryside and winery lunch

On the way to San Gimignano, admire the unique Tuscan landscape driving through marvelous postcard views surrounded by olive trees, vines and old stone buildings.

Next stop is at a family owned wine estate located in the typical Tuscan landscape with amazing views of vines and olive trees. Here you will have time to relax and enjoy a proper Tuscan lunch that gives you a taste of country hospitality.

The menu includes assorted cold cuts, cured ham, salami, cheeses, bruschetta, pasta, or soup, salad, dessert and coffee, paired with the winery's own excellent wine, olive oil and vin santo (sweet dessert wine).

San Gimignano

After lunch, we will reach San Gimignano and end the day in complete relax. Famous for its splendid skyline of tall Medieval towers, here you will have free time to explore the main street full of typical shops, have a look at the "Collegiata" (main church), or enjoy a gelato!

Your perfect day trip to Siena, San Gimignano & the Tuscan countryside ends with a comfortable ride back to Florence.

FAQ’s
What happens if the weather looks bad?
Rain or Shine Guarantee (Zero Risk)

In Tuscany, rainy days are rare, and our tours run beautifully regardless of the weather. Our modern, climate-controlled vehicles are fully enclosed and comfortable, and your authentic winery lunch and tastings take place inside cozy, historic cellars.

Maximum Flexibility & Peace of Mind

We pride ourselves on our flexibility and reasonable approach. Check the forecast up to 24 hours before your departure—if you see rain and prefer to switch dates, we will do everything possible to accommodate you on another day, completely free of charge. We are humans, not rigid corporations: your peace of mind is our priority.

How much walking is involved in this small group tour?

The itinerary involves moderate walking and a few stairs. It can be covered by all people on average fitness level.

Unfortunately, being a group tour it is not suitable for people with walking difficulties or not able to follow the standard pace of the group. If you'd like, we can help you with the private version of the Siena and San Gimignano tour.

Can we add the guided visit of Siena Cathedral on the day of the tour?

Surely you can, accordingly to last-minute availability. Simply ask to your guide

For the light lunch, can we have a vegetarian option?

Vegetarian and gluten-free can be catered for upon request at the time of booking.

How long does it take the guided walking tour in Siena?

Approx. 1 hour and 15 mins, and includes all the highlights of Siena from outside.

May I visit the interiors of Siena Cathedral?

Entrance fees of the Cathedral + guided visit can be purchased directly on the day at Euro 15.00 per person. If you are interested, please ask to your guide. The Cathedral is not accessible on Sundays (due to the Mass) or during celebrations.

Can I buy the Siena Cathedral entrance fee, directly on-site and visit on my own?

Surely you can, according to tickets availability, the queue to buy the tickets and/or enter the Cathedral. Our guides have priority access so you can maximize your time.

Will the guide be with us also in San Gimignano?

The guide will bring you to the very heart of San Gimignano, give you a quick introductory tour and then you will have free time to relax, buy souvenirs and have a gelato. San Gimignano is a very small village where you can easily stroll around on your own.

Can I join the tour with an infant?

Infants are welcome on this tour! Although they travel for free, they must be confirmed at the time of booking. Infants will seat on the parent’s lap.

You may find additional info on our FAQs page, on the Small Group Tours section

On the private tour, does the driver speak English?

Yes. Our private drivers speak fluent English and drive these roads every week: they will tell you what you are looking at, where to stop and where to eat. What they cannot do, by Italian law, is lead a guided tour inside the historic centres — that requires a licensed guide, and a licensed guide comes with the small group departure.

Can the private tour start later, or from my hotel?

Yes to both. There is no meeting point and no check-in on the private day: we collect you at your hotel or any address in Florence, at the time you choose. Nine in the morning is the usual start, but it is your call. Tell us the time when you book, or send a message afterwards.

We are more than 8. Can you still take us privately?

Yes — eight is the limit of one vehicle, not of the day. For a larger family or a group of friends we add a second car with its own English-speaking driver, and you travel together. Write to us on WhatsApp with the number of people and the date and we will send you the price.
